1. Overview

Welcome to All Documents Reader ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you install and use our mobile application on Android devices.

Core Commitment: All Documents Reader is built as an offline-first document viewer. We do NOT collect, upload, or sell your documents, scanned images, or personal files to any remote server.

2. Information Collection & Usage

We believe in data minimization. The app only accesses information necessary to provide document viewing, management, and scanning capabilities:

A. Information You Provide

  • Documents and Files: Files that you explicitly open, view, or scan through the application (such as PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Text, and Images). All file reading and rendering is performed locally on your device.
  • Customer Inquiries: If you contact us for technical support or feedback, we receive your email address and any message details you choose to share.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device & Diagnostics Information: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic information (such as device model, OS version, app crashes, and basic performance metrics) through Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose errors and improve app stability.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Anonymous advertising IDs (Google Advertising ID / GAID) processed by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements in the free version of the app.

3. Device Permissions

To deliver its features, the application requests the following Android system permissions:

📄 Storage & Media Access

READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA_IMAGES: Required to browse, list, and open user-selected documents and PDF files stored on your device.

📷 Camera

CAMERA: Used solely when you use the document scanner to capture document pages or extract text using OCR.

🌐 Internet Access

INTERNET: Required for loading advertisements (AdMob), verifying in-app purchases (Google Play), and sending crash reports (Firebase).

You can revoke these permissions at any time via your device's Settings > Apps > All Documents Reader > Permissions.

4. Document & File Privacy

Your documents may contain sensitive or confidential data. Here is how we safeguard them:

  • 100% Local Processing: Document rendering and page navigation are performed entirely on your device via built-in viewer libraries.
  • On-Device OCR & Text Recognition: When you scan documents or extract text from images, machine learning recognition is executed on-device using Google ML Kit. No scanned images or extracted text are uploaded to cloud servers.
  • No Background Transmission: The application does not read, index, or transmit files in the background without your explicit instruction.

6. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

If you purchase a premium upgrade (e.g., ad removal or pro features):

  • All transactions are processed directly and securely by the Google Play Store Billing System.
  • We do not collect, view, or retain your payment card details, bank information, or billing address.
  • We only receive confirmation of the purchase token to unlock the corresponding features in your app.

7. Data Security & Storage

We maintain industry-standard security practices to protect any configuration data stored locally on your device (such as your app preferences, favorite documents, or theme settings via SharedPreferences). Because your documents remain exclusively on your device, their security is also protected by your device's operating system security and screen lock credentials.

8. Children's Privacy

All Documents Reader is a general productivity tool and is not intended for or directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can immediately delete it.

9. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA Compliance)

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or California), you may have certain legal rights regarding your data:

  • Access & Portability: Since your documents are saved locally on your phone, you retain full access and control over them at all times.
  • Erasure / Deletion: You can delete any saved file, recent document history, or app preferences directly from the app or by clearing the app data in device settings.
  • Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising by visiting your Android device settings under Settings > Google > Ads.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in legal regulations, platform policies, or app functionality. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
